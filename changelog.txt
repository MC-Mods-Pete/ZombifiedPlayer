ZombifiedPlayer Changelog
=========================

2.0.1-NeoForge-mc1.21.1
-----------------------
Fixes
 - Zombified players did not count as undead: Smite, potions of Healing and Harming and the Wither now treat them like zombies.

2.0.0-NeoForge-mc1.21.1
-----------------------
New
 - Zombified players now have the full player model: the second skin layer (jacket, sleeves, pants) is shown, and players with a slim skin (Alex model) get slim arms.
 - With Entity Model Features installed, zombified players move like zombies: the built-in resource pack "Zombified Player: Shambling Animation" lets them shamble, reach for you and swipe with both arms. It is turned on at the first start and can be turned off under Options > Resource Packs.
 - New option naturalSpawning (default off): zombified players also spawn naturally in the dark wherever zombies spawn, with the skin of a random player from randomSkinPlayerNames. They drop their equipment only by chance and despawn like zombies, and sometimes have loot in their pockets. Spawn weight with naturalSpawnWeight, name tag with displayNameTagForNaturalZombifiedPlayers.
 - New spawn egg. Zombified players from the spawn egg, from spawners or from /summon without a player get the skin of a random player from the new list randomSkinPlayerNames.
 - New option spawnWhenDiedWhileInfected (default off, Contagion): a zombified player also spawns when an infected player dies from any cause.
 - New option ignoreKeepInventory (default off), see Changes.
 - Config screen in the mod list (Mods > Zombified Player > Config).
Changes
 - The game rule keepInventory is now respected: the player keeps everything and the zombified player appears without items. With ignoreKeepInventory the zombified player takes the items anyway.
 - Curios and Accessories items now follow the rules of these mods: items that stay with the player or are destroyed (e.g. Curse of Vanishing, keepCurios) no longer go to the zombified player.
 - The door option is renamed to zombifiedPlayersCanAlwaysBreakDoors: off = zombified players break doors only on Hard like zombies, on = on every difficulty. If you had changed the old option, set the new one again.
 - Zombie models from other resource packs (e.g. Fresh Animations, Bray's Zombie Overhaul) no longer change zombified players, so the player skin always fits.
 - Players without their own skin are shown as Steve or Alex (always the same for a player) with the matching arm width.
 - The green zombification marks are only drawn on the body, the left arm and leg have their own marks.
 - Zombified players are always adults.
Fixes
 - Items of the zombified player got lost or damaged (with doMobLoot off, in Peaceful, items in the hands, armor).
 - The options makeTheZombifiedPlayersStronger and zombifiedPlayersCanBreakDoors had no effect.
 - Zombified players from /summon with data or from spawners only had half health with makeTheZombifiedPlayersStronger.
 - /summon zombifiedplayer:zombified_player without data failed.
 - The player lost the items when another mod cancelled the death.
 - The spawn message was missing when death messages are turned off.
 - Skins were never fetched again after a failed attempt, and on offline servers zombified players always looked like Steve.
 - Transparent arms with slim skins.
 - The zombified player had no name (Jade, death messages).
 - A Totem of Undying in an Accessories slot did not prevent the zombified player.
 - Hardcore Revival: dying after a knockout by a zombie spawns a zombified player again.
 - Mutants and Zombies: death by Mutant Zombie now spawns a zombified player, death by Mutant Brute no longer does (as before: Blister Zombie, Zombie Brute, Split Head Zombie).
 - Items with Curse of Vanishing went to the zombified player, and items got lost with transferMainAndOffHandToZombifiedPlayer off.
 - The German translation was not loaded.
